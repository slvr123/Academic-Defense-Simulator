Dataset Collection

Leaf images were collected from eleven rice fields within Barangay Masagana over a six-week period spanning the vegetative and reproductive stages of the 2024 wet season crop. Photographs were captured using a Samsung Galaxy A54 at a resolution of 3000 by 4000 pixels. To ensure consistency, each leaf was detached and photographed flat against a white cardboard background under natural daylight between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM, with the camera held approximately 25 centimeters from the leaf surface.
