Moving to your experimental methodology, you've set a strict 70 dB static threshold for ambient noise during testing. How does your system maintain this threshold without adaptive noise gating, and what is your plan for the spectral leakage from ambient signals just below that limit?

Your answer

The 70 dB limit is a condition on the test room, not our only gate. Section 3.2.4 describes an adaptive noise floor, and an event has to clear both. Below 70 dB, the bandpass filter and GCC-PHAT's phase weighting do most of the work, but we haven't measured accuracy at different ambient levels yet.
