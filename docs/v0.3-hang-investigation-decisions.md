**Status:** CLOSED — 2026-07-11
**Outcome:** Non-repro across the full evidence-anchored hypothesis space.
The deterministic hang as described does not exist on current `main`
(`40cd0ff`). Gate converted from "root-caused and fixed" to "bounded and
observable" (Decision 2 below).
**Companion:** `docs/briefs/v0.3-hang-investigation-brief.md` (the protocol

## Finding

The bug on record — "browser sessions freeze deterministically at ~the 6th
sequential Gemini call" — could not be reproduced on current `main` under any
condition the accumulated evidence pointed to, including conditions
specifically constructed to trigger each standing hypothesis. Eight-plus
attempts across four mechanically distinct repro conditions all ran clean.

Two non-exclusive explanations, deliberately not adjudicated (no evidence
discriminates them):

1. The `threading.Lock` partial fix plus 0.3b's engine/UI extraction removed
   the remainder of the original defect, and "something further remains" was
   written down without a post-fix repro to back it.
2. The original observation partially misattributed an environment artifact.
   This session produced a live demonstration of how: run 1's wedged
   `ImportError` (a partially-initialized module stuck in a long-lived
   process's `sys.modules`, on a OneDrive-synced repo with a same-day file-
   resurrection incident) broke the app in a way that a less careful pass
   could have logged as "the known hang again."

Either way, the correct record is the same: **the founding premise of the
investigation — fixed-count determinism — is retired**, the same move as
Day 4 retiring the Day 1 `difficulty_delta` framing. Decided by evidence,
not reasoning.

## Decision 1 — Investigation closed; no further repro attempts

Every hypothesis with an evidentiary anchor has been tested under conditions
built to trigger it. Further attempts would be guessing without an anchor.
Any future recurrence re-opens this with the observability from Decision 2
as its starting evidence — not another blind hunt.

## Decision 2 — Gate conversion: "don't ship silent" replaces "don't ship until root-caused"

The hard gate before 0.3d and the deployment decision is satisfied by
shipping two guards instead of a fix:

1. **Timeout guard** around the per-turn LLM call path in the driver layer:
   a stuck request surfaces a clean user-facing error state ("something went
   wrong — refresh to continue"; session fails clean per 5a's rule) instead
   of an infinite spinner. Threshold generous — order of 120s — this is a
   tripwire against infinite silence, not a latency SLO.
2. **Thin permanent call-lifecycle logging** in the driver: timestamp,
   thread id, call counter, started/returned per LLM call. Light enough to
   ship; enough that a recurrence in real use produces evidence.

The `threading.Lock` **stays** — run 3 is direct evidence it absorbs real
rerun races.

## What this closure is worth (for the case study)

This is a deliberate portfolio beat, not a consolation note: a disciplined
negative result. The investigation protocol (instrument-first, enumerated
signatures, hard stops) was executed literally — the agent hard-stopped
twice rather than manufacture a fix, invalidated two of its own runs by
root-causing its own tooling, and the human closed the remaining conditions
manually. The premise was then retired on evidence and the risk converted
from an unbounded silent failure into a bounded observable one. That is the
"decided by evidence, not reasoning" standard applied to debugging — the
same discipline as the Day 4 scoring-model finding, pointed at a bug hunt.
