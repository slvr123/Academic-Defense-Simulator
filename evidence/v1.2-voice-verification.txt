DoD 6 — autoplay-block fallback
--------------------------------
The in-iframe Replay control speaks the question. Captured at the moment of the
click, in the real app:

    [{ "voice": "Google UK English Male", "pitch": 0.80, "rate": 0.94, "chars": 352 }]
    iframe synth speaking now: true
    iframe synth pending: false

A genuine autoplay block could not be triggered naturally here — the page had
already received user gestures by the time the component rendered, which is the
normal case in this app since a session cannot start without clicks. What is
proven is the property that matters: the control produces a real utterance
independently of the automatic attempt, and it does so from a click inside the
iframe, which is what grants that document its own user activation.

cut):

    (was speaking: true before the click)


DoD 7 — no-matching-voice fallback, forced
-------------------------------------------
MECHANISM: the probe injects an extra row, __forced_no_match__, built from the
real component and then rewritten to point at preferences that cannot exist:
    ["Definitely Not An Installed Voice", "Nor Is This One"]
with pitch 1.42 and rate 0.61 (values used by nothing else, so they cannot be
confused with a real profile).

Result, from a real browser:
    {"key":"__forced_no_match__","resolved":"(browser default)",
     "pitch":1.4199999570846558,"rate":0.6100000143051147}

The browser default voice was used AND pitch and rate were still applied. That
is Decision 4 step 3 and step 4 together, which is the pairing that matters:
step 4 is what keeps a panel from collapsing when step 2 resolves nothing.

Also asserted structurally in tests, because the no-match path is what most
visitors will actually get:
